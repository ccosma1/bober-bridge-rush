WHO IS ON THE BRIDGE

        
Reds are beaver-shaped and they come in packs. A fresh pack steps in between every pair of banners. Suds wear foam. Haulers carry a tub. Spitters, hairballs, leaves, and ducks show up on later spans. Stand nearer the banner you want. The middle takes the left one.

        
Twelve guns. Incisors bite in threes. The Gnasher spins up. Dam Bust fans the front rank. The Ring Saw skips. Pine Rail goes through a line. Red Darts snap. Storm Arc jumps. Maple Flamer is the one flamethrower. White Spike slows. Lodge Rockets, the Bell Lob, and the Gold Orb hit a crowd.

        
Four spans, forty bridges. Each one ends on a keeper. Baron wears a crown and a plunger. Tub is round and teal, with a duck on his head. Grunk is tall and green and swings a wrench. Last through the pack and beat that keeper.
